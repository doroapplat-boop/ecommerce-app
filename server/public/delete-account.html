Delete your GUZO SHOP account

Google Play requires an easy way to request account deletion for apps with user accounts.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open GUZO SHOP
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account
  3. Type DELETE and confirm

Option 2 — Email request

Send an email from the address on your account:

Email deletion request

We will delete your account and related personal data (cart, wishlist, addresses). Some order records may be retained if required by law.
