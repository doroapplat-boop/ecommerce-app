GUZO SHOP Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

GUZO SHOP (“we”, “us”) is an ecommerce shopping app for browsing products, placing orders, and managing delivery addresses.

Information we collect

  • Account information (name, email, phone) through Clerk authentication
  • Shipping addresses you save
  • Cart, wishlist, and order history
  • Payment proof images you upload
  • Basic device/session data for login and optional push notifications

How we use information

We use data to operate your account, process and deliver orders, provide support, improve the app, and send optional order-related notifications.

Sharing

We use service providers such as Clerk (login), hosting/database providers, and cloud image storage. We do not sell personal information. This app does not use AdMob or other advertising SDKs.

Retention & deletion

We keep data while your account is active and as needed for orders/legal reasons. You can request account deletion in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on our account deletion page.
